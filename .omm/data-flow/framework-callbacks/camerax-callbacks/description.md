The CameraX side of stage 0. `CameraXEngine` attaches the same `Telemetry` capture callback to the preview through `Camera2Interop.Extender.setSessionCaptureCallback`, so capture started, completed, failed and buffer-lost events reach the event ring as they do from Camera2. The engine wraps that callback once and also hands every completed result to `CameraXControls`, whose AE relock and long-press exposure watch read the AE state. A still adds the JPEG arrival (`still`) and `media_saved` with `yuvOffsetNs`, the gap between the JPEG and the analysis frame paired with it; a recording adds `recording_started`, `recording_finalized` and `video_saved` from Recorder's events. The `ImageAnalysis` analyzer (keep-only-latest) reports image arrival with the image's own timestamp, and the `CameraState` observer reports open and closed transitions. These callbacks carry the same limit as the Camera2 ones: they record when the app was notified, not when the hardware did the work.
